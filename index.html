Vertical Slice – Polished Prototype

Controls: A/D or ←/→ move, Space jump, Shift ground dash, E interact, R reset. Press any control once to enable SFX/BGM.

Collect each stage's relics, pull the joystick, and use the gate to clear all six levels.

Current Build Notes

The prototype now includes a three-heart HUD, temporary invulnerability after damage, respawning stompable enemies with countdowns, procedural BGM, gameplay-triggered crystal gate glow, and six relic-gated platforming levels.

Enemies are threats and optional stomp targets; progression is based on relic collection so enemy respawns cannot re-lock a lever objective.
